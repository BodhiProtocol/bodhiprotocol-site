## MoSCoW Isn't a Framework, It's a Fight You're Avoiding

Three stakeholders. Three requests. All three tagged "Must Have". Capacity for one.

MoSCoW didn't fail in that meeting. It was never actually used: the four letters got borrowed, and the rule that makes them mean something didn't.

## T+1 Settlement: The Deadline Shrank. The Money Still Has to Cross an Ocean.

In 2024 the US cut its stock settlement deadline in half. A market roughly a tenth its size had already finished doing the same, about sixteen months earlier.

The shorter deadline exposed a problem the old one had quietly absorbed: for a foreign investor, the money still has to cross an ocean.

## It Worked in UAT. Why Did It Fail in Production?

"It worked in UAT." "The same code went to production." Both true. The Cancel button still vanished.

Production sent an order status called ALLOCATED that UAT had never seen. Same code does not mean the same system.
