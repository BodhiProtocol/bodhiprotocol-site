Where "Five Whys" usually stops

Taiichi Ohno's welding-robot example: the chain only ends when it names a missing safeguard.

PROBLEM
The welding robot stopped mid-cycle
↓
WHY 1
A fuse blew from a circuit overload
← The easy stop: replace the fuse, line runs in 10 minutes
↓
WHY 2
The bearings were under-lubricated and locked up
↓
WHY 3
The oil pump wasn't circulating enough oil
↓
WHY 4
Its intake was clogged with metal shavings
↓
WHY 5
There was no filter on the intake
← Root cause: a missing safeguard, not a person
