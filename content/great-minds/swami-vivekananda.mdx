Most philosophers explain ideas.

Vivekananda converted them.

At the widest point of his mind sat one profound philosophical truth: each soul is potentially divine. But he did not leave that sentence suspended in the air. He forced it through the funnel of life until it became strength, service, education, and national awakening.

That is what makes him unusual. He took one of the most abstract ideas in Indian philosophy and compressed it until it became action.
