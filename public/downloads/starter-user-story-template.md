# Starter User Story Template

A structure for turning a vague request into a story a developer and tester could both build from — plus the readiness checklist to run before you call it done. See [First Time Writing a User Story](https://bodhiprotocol.com/ba-playbooks/first-time-writing-a-user-story) for the full worked example this template is built from.

## CONTEXT

What happens today?
Why does this change exist?

## BUSINESS PROBLEM

What problem are we solving?

## USER STORY

As a...
I want...
So that...

## BUSINESS RULES

1.
2.
3.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Given...
When...
Then...

## EXCEPTIONS

What happens when the normal flow fails?

## DATA REQUIREMENTS

What information is needed?

## DEPENDENCIES

Which systems, teams or services are involved?

## OUT OF SCOPE

What are we deliberately not doing?

## OPEN QUESTIONS

What still needs an answer?

---

## My First User Story Checklist

- [ ] I understand the actual problem
- [ ] I know who the user is
- [ ] I understand the outcome they need
- [ ] The story describes value, not just a requested solution
- [ ] Important business rules are captured
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable
- [ ] Important exceptions are considered
- [ ] Data expectations are understood
- [ ] Dependencies are visible
- [ ] Out-of-scope items are clear
- [ ] Open questions are visible
- [ ] Someone can understand the story without me sitting beside them
