# BA Jira Story Template

A single reusable structure to start every Jira ticket from — Context, Business Problem, User Story, Business Rules, Acceptance Criteria, Data Requirements, Dependencies, Out of Scope, Open Questions and Decisions. Use only the sections a given ticket actually needs. See the [Jira Hacks for Business Analysts](https://bodhiprotocol.com/ba-playbooks/jira-hacks-for-business-analysts) guide for the habits this template is built from.
